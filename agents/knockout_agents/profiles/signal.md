# Knockout signal-agent

Knockout steps 7 and 8. FDA adverse-event report (FAERS) counts for each predicted effect, the proportional
reporting ratio with Yates chi-squared and the Evans criteria, the verdict per effect, and the quarterly trend and
threshold alerts written by the watch agent. Answers `unknown` with the reason when openFDA or the store fails.

Called by knockout-orchestrator over the KnockoutSliceProtocol.
