# Knockout genetics-agent

Knockout steps 3 to 5. For each target, the genetic diseases in the IUIS catalog, the direction of each disease
(IUIS stated, Orphanet product 6, or the words of the disease name), the fixed direction rule (a drug that lowers a
protein maps only to its loss-of-function diseases), the frequent phenotypes, and their FDA reaction terms.
Developmental phenotypes are excluded. Answers `unknown` with the reason when a source fails.

Called by knockout-orchestrator over the KnockoutSliceProtocol.
