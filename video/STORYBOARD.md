# Knockout: the 3:00 demo video for LA Hacks AI Hackathon 2026

**Who watches it:** Devpost judges and the sponsor judges (Fetch.ai, Creao, Tiger Data, Gemini, Sentry, Vultr),
before and after the in-person rounds on Sunday 18 Oct (round 1 at 12:30 PDT). Hacking ends Sunday 12:00 PDT;
the plan is to submit by 11:30.

**Length: exactly 180.0 s.** Devpost allows 2 to 3 minutes and Fetch.ai asks for 3 to 5, so 3:00 is the only
length that meets both. `build.sh` checks it with `ffprobe` and fails the build otherwise.

The beat sheet is the one in `PLAN.md` ("Video, 3:00"). The full timed narration is `NARRATION.md`, generated
from `stage/script.json`.

## Visual system

- **Grounds:** the report page's palette. Label stock (`oklch(0.986 0.004 85)`) for the product scenes,
  printer's ink (`oklch(0.172 0.006 75)`) for the hook, the title and the close.
- **One accent:** the amber of a medicine bottle (`oklch(0.53 0.13 58)` on paper, `oklch(0.79 0.13 72)` on ink),
  used for the place where the record agrees with the genetics: the symptom and the boxed warning when they
  meet in the hook, the matched words, PRR 146, and the idea "chemical knockout".
- **The black rule of a boxed warning** is the one graphic device: the hook's warning box draws itself and
  turns amber when it meets the symptom.
- **Pending is a dashed box.** The report page marks an unknown verdict with a dashed square; every beat that is
  not real yet uses the same dashed border and a `PENDING` tag, so nobody can take it for a result.
- **Type:** IBM Plex Sans for everything read as prose (headlines 92–112 px, weight 500, tracking −0.04em),
  IBM Plex Mono for record IDs, file names and terminal output. The same faces as the page, copied from its build
  into `stage/fonts/` (SIL Open Font License).
- **Through-line:** the genotype mark `−/−` (both copies of a gene knocked out). It sits on `C5` in the hook,
  becomes the wordmark on the title, and closes the video.
- **Motion:** the kit's eases (entrances `cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1)`, moves `(.77,0,.175,1)`), 14–30 px rises,
  pushes between ink and paper, dissolves between paper scenes. Camera moves over full-page screenshots for the
  report page.
- **Footage is real:** full-page screenshots of the running report page at 2× (Playwright, `capture/capture.mjs`),
  one keystroke at a time for the search, and terminal output captured from real runs. The page is shown in a
  plain browser frame with its real path in the bar.
