Where the Classway video looked:

- Mixkit (https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/), under the Mixkit Stock Music Free License (https://mixkit.co/license/#musicFree). The track it shipped with came from here.
- Pixabay (https://pixabay.com/music/), under the Pixabay Content License (https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/): free for videos, including YouTube, with no attribution required. Many tracks are registered with YouTube Content ID, so YouTube may show a copyright claim after upload; it doesn't block the video, and it can be cleared by disputing with the Pixabay license.

Read the license page before using a track; terms change. Avoid AI-generated tracks when a competition's rules ask for that to be disclosed.
