Recording booth

Ready
Put on headphones, turn on your microphone, then press Record. Read each line when it turns white.

The captions in the video are the script: read each one as it appears. “The whole script” records the whole video in one pass. Picking one line plays from 3 seconds before it and drops just that line into the saved take. Either way the voice is mixed over the music and the captions are re-timed to how each line was said.
